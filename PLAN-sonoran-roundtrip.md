# Sonoran round trip: Phoenix to Tucson and back (draft, 2026-11-01)

Why this trip: Ayelet found the Utah temperatures too low. Target daytime highs near 50 F or warmer, as insulated from El Niño as possible, nowhere east of Dallas. In the three biggest El Niño Decembers the low desert showed no wet signal and ran 55-59 F; mountains and Texas/southern plains did not (see DECISIONS.md, 2026-11-01 later).

Already visited, so skipped: Sedona, Phoenix city sights, Saguaro NP, Death Valley, Joshua Tree, Grand Canyon, Teton/Jackson, the Dec 2023 New Mexico + Texas loop.

Party: Zahi, Ayelet, Tal from Dec 21; Shelly and Maia join Dec 24 at PHX. One six-seat vehicle, picked up and returned at PHX (no one-way fee). Everyone flies to and from PHX.

## Weather in the three biggest El Niño winters (Dec 21 to Jan 2, ERA5 reanalysis, runs a few degrees cold)

- Phoenix area 58 F high (63 normal), 36 F low. Ajo/Organ Pipe 58 F (63), 39 F low. Tucson 55 F (61), 35 F low.
- Bisbee-type elevation (5,400 ft) 47 F (54 normal), 27 F low, a little snow. This is the cold stop; it gets one night only and a swap to Tucson if a storm is forecast.
- Globe and Tonto NM were not measured; expect between the Phoenix and Tucson numbers.
- Late December is early in the storm season. NOAA's Oct 15 outlook still favors a wetter Southwest overall, so rain days are possible; the Apache Trail, Organ Pipe dirt roads, and the Tubac to Tombstone drive are the pieces that suffer.

## Christmas-week closures (from search results; confirm before booking)

- Closed Dec 25: Organ Pipe visitor center, Tumacacori NHP, Kartchner Caverns, Boyce Thompson Arboretum. The plan puts Dec 25 at Organ Pipe itself, which is open 24 h.
- Dec 24 half days: Kartchner (8 am to 2 pm last year's pattern), Boyce Thompson (to 3 pm in 2024). The plan avoids both on Dec 24.
- Kartchner tours sell out months ahead; book as soon as the calendar opens.

## Unverified or risky, check first

- Organ Pipe: a temporary closure order effective Aug 24, 2026 closes the crossover roads south of South Puerto Blanco Drive, Grey's Ranch Road, Camino de Dos Republicas, and a 150-ft strip north of the border fence. South Puerto Blanco Drive was listed open. Ajo Mountain Drive and Quitobaquito were not addressed. Call 520-387-6849.
- Apache Trail: sources conflict on whether the dirt stretch past Tortilla Flat is open; the plan only needs the paved part to Tortilla Flat and Canyon Lake. Check ADOT or 511.
- Bisbee Queen Mine and Tombstone hours: sources disagree, call (Queen Mine 520-432-2071).
- Not checked at all: Tonto NM hours and cliff-dwelling tour rules, Lost Dutchman, Besh Ba Gowah, Desert Museum, Pima, and San Xavier holiday hours, all lodging availability and prices, flights to PHX.
- Lodging found: Sonoran Desert Inn in Ajo (about $125 a night in one listing, 520-373-0804). Others not yet researched.
- Border Patrol checkpoints exist on roads near the border (I-19 near Tubac is the known one). Carry passports or IDs.

## Swaps

- Rain on Dec 22: skip the Apache Trail, do Boyce Thompson and Globe only.
- Cold or snow forecast for Bisbee: do Tombstone and Tumacacori as a day trip from Tucson, drop the Bisbee night.
- Want more room: Chiricahua NM is 1 h 45 m from Bisbee but at 5,400 ft with about 12 inches of snow a season; not in the plan.
